# Conceptvisuals

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool op 11 oktober 2026. Het zijn conceptbeelden voor deze website, niet de oorspronkelijke advertenties van Chocolate Planet of Café Retro. Typografie wordt als toegankelijke HTML over de beelden gezet. De output is teruggebracht tot lokale WebP-bestanden voor het web (samen circa 244 kB); de originele PNG’s zijn bewaard.

## Chocolade

Bestand: `public/assets/chocolate-concept.webp` (1000 × 1250). Origineel: `docs/original-concepts/chocolate-concept.png`.

Exacte prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create a high-end photographic campaign background for a Dutch personal marketing studio website. Portrait 4:5 composition. Extreme tactile close-up of sculptural stacked dark chocolate shards and a single broken truffle, natural cocoa dust, on burnt orange terracotta plinth against a monochrome warm coral-orange seamless studio background. Strong late-afternoon side light, rich deep brown glossy highlights, long editorial shadow. The chocolate occupies lower two-thirds with spacious empty orange upper third for typography that will be added in code. Sophisticated independent food magazine art direction, analog medium-format grain, realistic commercial food photography, unexpected asymmetric composition. No packaging, no branding, no text, no letters, no watermark. This is a concept campaign visual, not an actual client advertisement.

## Café

Bestand: `public/assets/cafe-concept.webp` (1000 × 1250). Origineel: `docs/original-concepts/cafe-concept.png`.

Exacte prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Portrait 4:5 high-end editorial campaign photo for an independent Dutch café. Cinematic close crop of two hands clinking two short elegant coupe cocktail glasses with amber drinks and orange peel, in a moody warmly lit brown cafe at golden hour. No faces. Beautiful hard afternoon sunlight falls across a walnut table, honest little reflections and slight film grain, background out of focus olive green and deep brown. Drinks occupy bottom two thirds, top third is dark quiet olive interior with room for white overlaid text that will be added in code. Intimate personal spontaneous atmosphere, analog 35mm lifestyle commercial photograph, natural skin and anatomical hands, sophisticated not generic stock photography. No logos, no text, no watermark. Concept visual not an actual client project.
